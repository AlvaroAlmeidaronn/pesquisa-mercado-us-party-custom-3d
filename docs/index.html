The Studio Process

How your pet becomes a keepsake

Commitment is a photo. Reciprocity is a preview. Nothing is made until you say yes.

  1. 01

    Message the atelier

    WhatsApp your pet type, the occasion (birthday cake, wedding, or keepsake only), and your event date if you have one.

  2. 02

    Send 1–3 clear photos

    Everyday phone photos with natural light work best. Front, side, and coat details. No heavy filters needed.

  3. 03

    Approve the digital preview

    We sculpt and send you a digital preview. If an ear angle or coat patch needs adjusting, we refine it before crafting.

  4. 04

    Handcrafted & delivered

    Hand-finished with archival matte pigments. Safely boxed and shipped directly to your hands in time for your date.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about the craft and process.

What kind of photos should I send?

Any 1 to 3 clear photos taken with your phone under natural light. A full-body view, a clear face shot, and any special markings (spots, unique tail, coat gradient) work perfectly.

Will I see what it looks like before you make it?

Yes, 100%. We always provide a digital preview first. Production only begins after you give your explicit approval.

Is this only for cake toppers or can I order just a keepsake?

Both! While many people love having their pet on their birthday or wedding cake, over half of our orders are made purely as home keepsakes to keep on a desk or shelf.

How long does the entire process take?

Because each piece is custom designed, crafted, and painted by hand, timing depends on our current studio queue and your event date. Tell us your date on WhatsApp and we will confirm scheduling upfront.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is transparent and based on pet size, detail complexity, and whether you'd like single or multiple pets. Send a photo and we’ll reply with a custom quote.
