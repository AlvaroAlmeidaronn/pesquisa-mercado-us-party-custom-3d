## 1. Resumo em português

### Em uma frase
A página vende: **“foto do seu pet → você aprova a prévia → fabricamos → festa e/ou prateleira”**.  
Não vende: “impressora 3D” nem “fazemos qualquer coisa”.

### Por que não só “pet no bolo”?
Porque bolo **não** é o maior mercado — foi só a porta de entrada.  
O mecanismo único é o que diferencia; o bolo é onde a foto fica mais bonita.

### Dissensos abertos
- WhatsApp nos EUA (pessoas podem sumir no chat)  
- Memorial **fora** do MVP por enquanto (tom + stress)  
- Variante “cake-first” só depois, em teste A/B — **não** duas histórias na mesma página

- Preço fixo · depoimento inventado · “só restam 2” · entrega amanhã · jargão de impressora no topo

### 4.7 Objeção central (nomear em voz alta)

**Section title:** The real worry (we hear you)

**“I’m worried it won’t look like my pet — and I don’t want another generic decoration.”**

That’s exactly why we use *your* photos and send a **preview first**. If the likeness isn’t right, we revise before anything is made. We don’t ask you to hope for the best.

**Other honest lines:**

**“I’ve seen cheap name toppers online.”**  
Acrylic letters are great for names. This is different: a small sculpted figurine of *your* pet — for the cake and/or the shelf. If you only need a name plaque, this isn’t the right product.

**“How much / how long?”**  
Timing depends on your date and queue. Price depends on size and details after we see your photos. Message us with the date (or “keepsake only”) + photos — we’ll confirm timing and send a **quote** after we plan your preview.

### 4.8 FAQ

**Q1. What do I need to start?**  
Clear photo(s), pet type (dog/cat), occasion or “keepsake only”, and event date if you have one. Start on WhatsApp.

**Q2. Do you make it before I approve?**  
No. Digital preview first. Production only after you approve.

**Q3. Is this only for cake toppers?**  
Cake is the most popular use — but the same figurine can be a keepsake with no party. Same process.

**Q4. How long does it take?**  
It varies. Share your date early so we can say honestly whether we can make it. We don’t fake “ships tomorrow.”

**Q5. How does pricing work?**  
No hard price on this page. After photos + what you want (size/style), we send a quote. You decide before we produce.
